Recycled Vegetal
Clumping Litter

Sanicat Recycled is a recycled, natural, biodegradable, and compostable clumping litter. It comes from the recovery of non-food derivatives from the corn farming industry.

It creates clumps for easy cleaning and naturally neutralises unpleasant odours, allowing the litter to remain clean for a longer time.

It doesn’t produce dust and its fine-grained texture make it soft to the touch for sensitive paws. It is also free from added dyes, perfumes, or harmful ingredients and its natural origin is 100% preserved.
